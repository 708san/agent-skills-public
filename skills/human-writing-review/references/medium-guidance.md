# Medium Guidance

Load this reference only when the medium is known and its conventions materially affect the edit.

These rules adapt the same review-and-revision responsibility; they do not expand the Skill into strategy, research, SEO, or content planning.

## note / editorial articles

Favor:

- a visible point of view
- uneven emphasis that follows the argument
- concrete observations supported by the source
- paragraph rhythm shaped by the writer's reasoning

Watch for:

- generic introductions
- tidy conclusions that merely summarize everything
- overly balanced sections that dilute the writer's position

## Notion / internal documents

Favor:

- clarity and scanability
- explicit decisions, owners, conditions, or next actions when supplied
- headings and lists when they improve retrieval

Watch for:

- ceremonial prose
- repeated explanation
- headings or lists that add structure without helping retrieval

## Landing pages

Favor:

- the reader's problem
- concrete benefits tied to supplied information
- proof and conditions only when they are present in the source
- a clear action when the source provides one

Watch for:

- abstract value claims detached from user outcomes
- invented proof, customer results, performance claims, or urgency

Do not turn the task into conversion strategy, market research, or offer design unless separately requested.

## Business writing

Favor:

- a clear actor
- the decision or request
- the reason
- the next action
- deadline or condition when supplied

Watch for:

- ornamental phrasing
- excessive politeness that obscures the request
- duplicated context

## Owned-media articles

Favor:

- useful specificity
- credible scope
- a clear editorial thesis
- claims proportionate to the supplied support

Watch for:

- generic SEO-style padding
- repetitive section summaries
- claims broader than the available evidence

Do not perform keyword research, SEO optimization, source research, or fact-checking as part of this Skill.
